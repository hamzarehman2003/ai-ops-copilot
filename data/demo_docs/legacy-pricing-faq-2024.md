# Tasklane Pricing FAQ

*Last updated: March 2024*

## Which plans does Tasklane offer?

Tasklane has three plans: Free, Pro, and Enterprise.

## How much does Pro cost?

Pro costs $10 per seat per month billed annually, or $12 per seat per month billed monthly.

## Is single sign-on included in Pro?

Yes. Pro workspaces can connect a SAML identity provider such as Okta or Azure AD and enforce
single sign-on for all members. Enterprise adds SCIM provisioning on top of SSO.

## How many guests can I invite?

Pro workspaces can invite up to 5 guests per project. Enterprise has unlimited guests.

## What happens when I hit my automation limit?

On Pro you get 5,000 automation runs per month. After that, additional runs are billed at
$0.01 per run on your next invoice; automations are never paused.

## Can I get a refund?

Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of purchase.

## What is the API rate limit?

All paid plans share a limit of 120 requests per minute.
