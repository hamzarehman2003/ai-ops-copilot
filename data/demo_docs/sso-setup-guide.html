Set up SAML single sign-on

Applies to: Enterprise plan · Last reviewed: 2026-02-10 · Version 3.0

SAML single sign-on (SSO) lets members of an Enterprise workspace sign in through your company identity provider (IdP). SSO is not available on Free or Pro plans. Only workspace Owners can configure SSO.

Before you begin

  • Your workspace must be on the Enterprise plan or an active Enterprise trial.
  • You must be a workspace Owner.
  • You must verify at least one email domain under Settings → Domains.
  • You need admin access to your identity provider (Okta, Entra ID, OneLogin, or any SAML 2.0 provider).

Step 1: Create the Tasklane app in your IdP

  1. In your IdP, create a new SAML 2.0 application named "Tasklane".
  2. Set the ACS URL to https://auth.tasklane.example/saml/acs/<workspace-id>.
  3. Set the Entity ID to https://auth.tasklane.example/saml.
  4. Map the email attribute to the user's primary email address.

Step 2: Connect the IdP in Tasklane

  1. Go to Settings → Security → Single sign-on.
  2. Upload the IdP metadata XML, or paste the SSO URL and X.509 certificate.
  3. Click Test connection. A successful test signs you in through the IdP in a new tab.

Step 3: Enforce SSO

After a successful test, turn on Require SSO for all members. Members with emails on a verified domain will be redirected to the IdP at their next sign-in. Owners always keep a password-based break-glass login in case the IdP is unavailable.

Troubleshooting

Error codeMeaningFix
SSO-401 The SAML assertion signature is invalid. Re-upload the current IdP certificate. Certificates rotated in the IdP are not picked up automatically.
SSO-403 The user's email domain is not verified for this workspace. Verify the domain under Settings → Domains, or add the user as a guest.
SSO-404 SSO is not enabled for this workspace. Check that the workspace is on Enterprise. SSO is turned off automatically when an Enterprise trial ends or the workspace downgrades.
SSO-409 The email is already linked to another Tasklane workspace that enforces SSO. The user must be removed from the other workspace, or use a different email.
SSO-429 Too many sign-in attempts from the same user. Wait 15 minutes and try again.
