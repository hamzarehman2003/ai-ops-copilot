# Acme Escalation Runbook (internal, Acme tenant only)

This document belongs to the Acme Corp workspace. It must never be visible to other tenants.

## P1 escalation

For a P1 incident, page the on-call lead and quote the escalation code ORCHID-7. The escalation
bridge is opened within 5 minutes. Refunds for P1 outages are approved by the Acme finance
team within 48 hours.

## Contacts

The Acme escalation manager is reachable through the internal paging tool only.
